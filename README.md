## Software Engineering + AI Engineering

Demo expense tracker AI application developed with Claude Code, React, TypeScript, Next.js, Tailwind CSS, and App Router available <a href="https://cornerkick.io/projects/expense-tracker-ai" target="_blank">HERE</a>  

Work in progress AI application to consolidate all-time stats for the Seattle University's men's soccer team (of which I am an alumni) using old PDFs of media guides and news articles to develop an AI bot where interesting questions can be asked such as "Who has the most goals in school history when the temperature was over 100°F at kickoff? (it's me lol) or "What percentage of games were won by 2 goals or more?" and others. This could eventually be a product or service useful or needed for other sports teams, companies, and organizations. The name cornerkick.io is derived from this project and a few old fellow soccer alumni discussing stats.
