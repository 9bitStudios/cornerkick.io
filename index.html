RetroStats AI

Work in progress AI application to consolidate all-time stats for the Seattle University's men's soccer team (of which I am an alumni) using old PDFs of media guides to develop an AI bot where interesting questions can be asked such as "Who has the most goals in school history when the temperature was over 100°F at kickoff? (it's me lol) or "Who has the most assists in the month of November in school history?" and others. It's a work in progress but I'm hoping to roll this into a product or service that can be sold to other college and youth programs because it is fun and would be fun for teams' end-of-year banquets and other events. This is where the name cornerkick.io comes from because it started with the soccer stats idea a few old fellow soccer alumni had been discussing.
